Scrambled eggs
Breakfast · Yields 262 portions · Base: 50 portions
Contains: Egg, Dairy Breakfast ⌄
Scale recipe for
portions
Base recipe is for 50 portions. Adjust number above to auto-scale all quantities.
Ingredients (scaled to 262 portions)
Eggs, large EGG655 eggs (~22 doz)
Whole milk DAIRY5.5 qts
Butter, unsalted DAIRY2.2 lbs
Salt3 tbsp
Black pepper1.5 tbsp
Chives (optional)1 bunch
Method
1
Crack eggs into large mixing bowls. Add milk, salt, and pepper. Whisk until fully combined and slightly frothy.
2
Heat large hotel pans or griddle to medium-low. Melt butter across cooking surface.
3
Pour egg mixture in and cook low and slow, gently folding with a spatula every 30 seconds. Do not scramble aggressively.
4
Remove from heat while eggs appear slightly underdone — they will continue cooking. Garnish with chives if using. Hold in 140°F steam table.
Roast chicken with pan jus
Dinner · Yields 262 portions · Base: 50 portions
No major allergens Dinner ⌄
Scale recipe for
portions
Base recipe is for 50 portions.
Ingredients (scaled to 262 portions)
Chicken, bone-in pieces~130 lbs (5.25 lbs/10 portions)
Canola oil1.5 qts
Garlic powder½ cup
Onion powder½ cup
Paprika¼ cup
Salt½ cup
Black pepper3 tbsp
Chicken stock3 gal (pan jus)
Method
1
Pat chicken dry. Combine all dry seasonings. Coat each piece generously with oil then season all over.
2
Arrange in hotel pans skin-side up. Do not overlap. Roast at 375°F until internal temp reaches 165°F, approx 45–55 min.
3
Deglaze pans with chicken stock. Reduce slightly for pan jus. Season to taste.
4
Hold chicken at 140°F minimum. Serve pan jus alongside in labeled containers.
Pasta Bolognese
Dinner · Yields 262 portions · Base: 50 portions
Contains: Gluten, Dairy, Egg Dinner ⌄
Scale recipe for
portions
Ingredients (scaled to 262 portions)
Pasta, penne GLUTEN52 lbs dry
Ground beef, 80/2065 lbs
Crushed tomatoes, canned13 #10 cans
Onions, diced20 lbs
Garlic, minced2 lbs
Olive oil1.5 qts
Parmesan, grated DAIRY8 lbs (garnish)
Italian seasoning½ cup
Method
1
Sauté onions and garlic in olive oil until soft. Add ground beef and brown, breaking up well. Drain excess fat.
2
Add crushed tomatoes and seasoning. Simmer minimum 45 minutes, stirring regularly. Adjust salt.
3
Cook pasta in large batches to al dente. Drain, toss lightly with oil to prevent sticking. Hold separately from sauce.
4
Serve pasta then sauce over the top. Parmesan on side — note: contains dairy allergen.
