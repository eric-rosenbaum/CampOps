# Everything the New York packet asks for

A page-by-page survey of **every document in the catalog**, made by rendering each PDF and
reading it. Not derived from the coordinate maps: only five documents have a map, and the point
of this survey is the other seventeen. Where a claim here disagrees with a map, the paper wins —
this was read off the page.

Method: `pdftoppm -png -r 110` over all 13 bundled PDFs (70 pages), every page opened and read.
Written 2026-08-31, against the files in `public/forms/ny/` as bundled on 2026-08-29. Sources and
revision dates are in `public/forms/ny/SOURCES.md`.

The catalog holds **22 documents in 13 files** — several county documents are page ranges inside
one application packet. Companion to `form-field-inventory.md`, which counts mapped cells on the
five forms we fill; this one is about what is *asked*, everywhere.

## 1 · DOH-367 — Children's Camp Facility and Staff Description
`doh-367.pdf` · 2 pages · rev 1/12 · mapped (280 cells) · **we fill this**

Instructions say: submit to the LHD **at least 60 days prior to camp operation**; information not
available is written as "Pending"; for expired certifications you may list the date of a
scheduled re-certification course.

**Facility.** Facility name · facility code · date open · date close · *"Are 20% or more of the
campers developmentally disabled?"* (Yes/No).

**Activities available to campers** — 25 tick boxes, six of them starred ("please further specify
the activity in the space provided", 6 write-on rules): Amusement Parks, Aquatic Theme Parks,
Archery, Arts and Crafts, Bicycling, Boating/Canoeing/Rafting, Camp Trips, Classroom Instruction,
Cooking, Dancing/Acting, Gymnastics, High Adventure\*, Hiking, Horseback Riding, Ice Skating,
Martial Arts, Mountain Boarding, Nature Study, Organized Games (Play), Petting Zoo, Riflery,
Roller Skating/Blading, Ropes/Challenge Course, Skate Boarding, Sports, Swimming – On-Site,
Swimming – Off-Site, Swimming – Wilderness, Other Water Activities\*, Other\*.

**Camper capacity** — 10 session rows × (Day / Overnight tick · number of days · male+female
counts for age bands 1–5, 6&7, 8–12, 13–15, 16&17, and CITs) = 12 counts per row. Header says
**use actual attendance from last season**; a tick box declares the figures are estimates because
the camp did not operate. Footnote: a CIT must be 15 at a day camp, 16 or 17 at an overnight camp.

**Camp director.** Name · date of birth · education · qualifying experience. Note on the page:
LDSS-3370 and DOH-2271 must be completed by the camp director and submitted with this form.

**Camp health director.** Name(s) · qualification tick (Doctor, Nurse Practitioner, Physician
Assistant, RN, LPN, EMT, Other + write-in) · NYS licence number · *day camps only*: on-site or
off-site.

**Health director certifications** — CPR and First Aid, each with: who holds it (Health Director /
Assistant tick), course provider, course title, issue date.

**Aquatics director.** Name · date of birth · five certification rows (Lifeguard Supervision and
Management\*, Lifeguarding, Progressive Swimming Instructor, CPR\*, First Aid), each with course
provider, course title, issue date; starred two are required to qualify. Plus **aquatic
experience**, one of three ticks: one season as a NYS camp aquatics director / two seasons
cumulatively ≥12 weeks as a camp lifeguard where more than one guard supervised / ≥18 weeks as a
lifeguard where more than one guard supervised.

**Written safety plan** — one of three: plan attached · previously submitted on \_\_/\_\_/\_\_ and
still current · update to plan attached.

**Facility additions/modifications** — list attached · no additions/modifications · not applicable
(camp did not operate last season). The page spells out what counts: buildings (cabins, kitchens,
dining halls, infirmary, assembly areas, privies and toilets), potable water and sewage disposal,
pools, beaches, activity areas (challenge course, archery and rifle ranges), emergency access and
egress roads.

**Itinerary of camp trips** — list attached · no trips. Asks for activities and trip dates.

**Camper rights brochure** — a statement/brochure submitted to DOH and approved · or the DOH
brochure "Children's Camps in New York State" (#3601).

**Attestation.** "I certify that the information given in this form is true." Signature of camp
operator · print name · title · date.

## 3 · DOH-2040 — Children's Camp Written Plan Checklist

Not a form that collects data — an index to the camp's written plan. Instruction to the operator:
"Use the following checklist to determine if your written plan addresses the requirements of
Subpart 7-2… Activities not provided by your camp should be checked N/A. **All other items
listed, including those already shaded in the N/A column, must be addressed in your plan.**
Please submit the completed checklist with your written plan or plan revision."

Header: camp name · date · county · address.

Every row has five columns: **Page** and **Yes** and **N/A** (camp operator completes), then
**Local Health Department Remarks**, then **Plan Segment Acceptable Yes/No** (department). On
some rows the N/A cell is pre-shaded — those components are mandatory and may never be ruled out.

**76 components** in eight groups:

- **Table of contents** (1)
- **Personnel** (3): chain of command · job description · qualification/reference verification
- **Facility operation** (8): water supply · on-site sewage treatment system(s) · lightning risk
  assessment · transportation · housing · food protection · general operation/maintenance ·
  waterfront facility maintenance
- **Fire safety** (8): evacuation plans and assembly area · fire prevention · electrical safety ·
  alarm system and smoke detectors · fire extinguishers · exits and exit signs · fire drills and
  log · submitted to local fire department
- **Medical plan** (11): duties of health director/personnel · camp infirmary description ·
  medication storage/administration · universal precautions · routine health care/surveillance ·
  emergency/outbreak procedures · camper medical history/screening · existing health
  conditions/restrictions · medical log · illness, injury and abuse reporting · camp sanitation
- **Activities/supervision** (17): general supervision and discipline · passive activity
  supervision · supervision during rest/sleep time · between activity supervision · supervision
  during transportation · supervision in emergencies · swimming · buddy system · off-site and
  wilderness swimming · stream crossing/incidental immersion · boating · horseback riding ·
  rope/challenge course · archery · riflery · out-of-camp trips · other activity plans
- **Staff training** (17): outline of curriculum · tour of camp · description of camp hazards ·
  chain of command · supervision and discipline · child abuse recognition and reporting · first
  aid/emergency medical response · injury and illness reporting · buddy system · lost swimmer
  plan · lost camper plan · out-of-camp trips · lightning plan · fire safety/fire drill
  procedures · camp evacuation procedures · activity specific training · **training attendance
  documentation**
- **Camper orientation** (10): outline of curriculum · tour of camp · description of camp hazards
  · reporting of illness and injury incidents · buddy system · lost camper plan · fire drills and
  evacuation · out-of-camp trips · lightning plan · **orientation attendance documentation**

Footer, twice over: Completed by (camp operator) + date, and **Revisions Added by** (camp
operator) + date; each with an LHD "Reviewed by / Date / Approved Yes–No (circle one)" block.

## 4 · DOH-2271 — Prospective Children's Camp Director Certified Statement
`doh-2271.pdf` · 1 page · rev 3/06 · mapped (44 cells) · **we fill this**

Banner: "THIS STATEMENT IS RELATIVE TO CONVICTION OF A CRIME OR THE EXISTENCE OF A PENDING
CRIMINAL ACTION."

Name (children's camp director) · date of birth · home address (street, city, state, zip).

One question: *"Have you ever been convicted of a crime (i.e., a misdemeanor or a felony) or do
you presently have a criminal action pending against you?"* Yes/No.

If yes, for **each** conviction or pending action, eight items: date of the incident · date of the
conviction or charge · the crime · the nature of the incident · city, county and state · name of
the court · penalties imposed (fine, jail term, restitution) · and for each penalty, the date it
was complied with — two rows of (date of fine · restitution paid in full Yes/No · date jail term
completed).

Certification "under penalty of perjury that the above information is complete and accurate":
print name · signature of children's camp director · date.

**This is personal criminal-history data about a named individual.** It belongs with the
never-upload set alongside background checks, not in a document store.

## 6 · DOH-1323 — Report on Operation of Swimming Pool
`doh-1323.pdf` · 2 pages · rev 10/04 (GEN-148) · **no map** · a recurring operating log

A **monthly** log sheet, mailed to the health department at the end of each month. Header: name of
pool · county · city/village/town · month and year.

One row per day, 31 rows, each with: filter washed check · pool cleaned check · **total number of
bathers** · chlorine used (lbs/day, gals/day, or crock — tick) · **three disinfectant tests**
(1st, 2nd, 3rd), each with time, free and total residual mg/l · alkalinity mg/l CaCO₃ · pH · pool
drain visible check · acid (quarts/pounds tick) · soda ash (pounds) · other · remarks.

A disinfection header tick chooses **chlorine or bromine**. Footer: source of water · "\*Pints of
\_\_ % chlorine in \_\_ gallons of water" · operator's signature · date.

Page 2 is instruction, and is the operating standard behind our pool module: residual must be
checked **at least three times a day**, especially before and after heavy bathing; for pH 7.2–7.8
free chlorine minimum 0.6 mg/l (spas 1.5) and maximum 5 mg/l; bromine minimum 1.5 mg/l (spas 3.0),
maximum 6; for pH 7.8–8.2 free chlorine minimum 1.5 mg/l; **chlorine compounds containing cyanuric
acid are not acceptable**; chlorine and bromide measured by the **DPD method**; sample taken
between inlet and outlet at about 12" depth; unusual circumstances (pump failure, GFI testing,
cloudiness) go in remarks; the health department must be **immediately notified** of any equipment
change, treatment interruption, loss of water clarity, or serious injury.

## 7 · DOH-2287 — Daily Report on Beach Operation
`doh-2287.pdf` · 2 pages · rev 5/04 · **no map** · a recurring operating log

The beach counterpart, also monthly-per-sheet, completed daily. Header: name of beach · address
(city, village, town) · county · month · year.

One row per day, 31 rows, seven columns: **number of lifeguards on duty** · **daily number of
bathers** · weather conditions (coded S/C/R/W, and **air temperature** per the instructions) ·
water quality monitoring (sample results under the date collected, plus visual observations —
oils, greases, sewage discharges, and whether clarity is sufficient to see an object at four feet)
· **number of rescues** (each described on the reverse) · **number of injuries** (each described on
the reverse) · remarks, such as beach closure due to weather or clarity.

Footer on both pages: operator's name · signature · date. Page 2 carries the instructions and a
ruled comments area for the rescue and injury descriptions.

## 8 · DOH-1309 — Engineering Report for Swimming Pool Plans
`doh-1309.pdf` · 4 pages · rev 1/93 · **no map** · **not the camp's to fill**

Design compliance with Subpart 6-1, submitted with plans when a pool is **built or changed** —
"(Check One) New Pool / Change to Existing Pool" — and **signed by the designing engineer or
architect**, with their address, PE/architect licence number or seal, and telephone number. The
form's own note says it supplements the engineer's narrative report in the transmittal of plans.

Fourteen sections, all physical-plant engineering: general (owner, name, city/county, pool type
across ten kinds including spa, wading, white water slide, wave, movable bottom; construction start
and completion dates) · pool configuration (construction type, length, width, area, shape, minimum
and maximum depths, capacity in gallons, transition slope) · bather capacity, by the stated formula
· water supply (drinking, sanitary, pool source, quantity gpm, fill pipe, back-siphonage
prevention) · deck equipment (ladders, disabled access, diving boards, deck slide, 4" stripe, depth
markers, fencing height and openings, self-closing gates, latch height, elevated lifeguard chairs,
step and stair dimensions) · recirculation equipment (pump make/model, turnover computation, pipe
sizes and velocities, hair catcher, vacuum, filters and filtration/backwash rates, gauges, rate
controllers, flow meter, inlets) · pool waste drain (pipe, grates, emptying time, backflow
prevention, main drain, gutter, surge capacity, skimmers, deck drains) · chemical feeders and test
equipment (disinfectant, storage, feeder make/capacity/strength/dosage/point of application, test
kit ranges for alkalinity/hardness, chlorine residual and pH, pH control chemical, automatic
deactivation device) · waste disposal · bathhouse fixture counts (showers, lavatories, toilets,
urinals, by sex) · lifesaving equipment (lifeguard chairs, reaching pole, pocket mask, ring buoys or
rescue tube, spine board, first aid kit, first aid room, chlorine gas storage and SCBA) ·
electrical and ventilation (underwater lights, deck junction box, Underwriters' certificate,
illumination, GFCIs) · spas · water slides and special purpose pools.

## 9 · DOH-2436 — Engineering Report for Bathing Beaches
`doh-2436.pdf` · 2 pages · rev 1/16 · **no map** · **not the camp's to fill**

The beach counterpart, design compliance with Subpart 6-2, again **signed by the designing engineer
or architect** with licence number or seal, for a new beach or a change to an existing one. Header
also asks for the **name and location of the watershed**. Every row carries its **code section**
reference (6-2.19 x.x) and a Yes/No/N.A. + comments column marked FOR OFFICE USE ONLY.

Eight subjects: bathers use (shallow area ÷ 25, deep area ÷ 75, total) · bottom slopes (max 1:10
shallow, 1:3 deep) · areas and area designations (water body surface acres, land area, min 35 sq ft
per bather; floatlines for wading, shallow and deep areas; marker buoy spacing; diving board height
and water depths) · water supply (drinking water source, number of fountains, dilution water, gpd
per bather) · water quality (clarity in feet, bacteriological TC/FC, anticipated currents) ·
bathhouse (distance from beach, fixture counts by sex, hose bibs with vacuum breakers) · electrical
(overhead wire clearance, National Electrical Code conformity) · safety provisions (warning sign
size and letter height, depth markers, and an equipment checklist: 24-unit first aid kit, spine
board, pocket mask, rescue board or boat, patrol boats/offshore stations, rescue tube or torpedo
buoy with 6-foot line, rescue can with 500-foot hauling line, life vests; an emergency care room for
beaches over 500 bather capacity, with potable water, cot, blankets, advanced first aid supplies and
resuscitation equipment; number and location of elevated lifeguard chairs).

Attachments it demands: **results of sanitary survey** and **bacteriological test results (8
weeks)**.

## 10 · WCDOH — Certification that Bathing Facility Safety Plans Are Up-To-Date
`wcdoh-bathing-plan-certification.pdf` · 2 pages · rev 4/25 · **no map**

The bathing-facility equivalent of DOH-367's "previously submitted and still current" box, and the
strongest evidence in the packet for how New York expects safety plans to work in practice: page 1
is a notice explaining that a facility whose conditions are **unchanged** may file this
certification **in place of resubmitting the safety plans**. Revised plans are required only if
alterations make the approved plans incomplete or non-representative.

Page 2 is the form itself: date · bathing facility name · **permit number** · then two independent
blocks, each "complete if applicable":

- **Swimming pool safety plan** — name, title, "hereby certify according to … §6-1.23(c) … that
  there are no changes or additions to procedures, for the operation and/or maintenance of the
  above referenced facility for the \_\_\_\_ bathing season", signature.
- **Bathing beach safety plan** — the same, citing §6-2.17(c).

## 11 · WCDOH — Original Permit to Operate a Bathing Facility (application package)
`wcdoh-bathing-facility-application.pdf` · 7 pages (one intentionally blank) · **no map**

**Pages 1–2, notice to applicants.** File **no later than 30 days prior to operation**. A separate
completed application **for each pool** at the facility. **Only the owner may sign** — execution by
a managing agent is refused. Corporate ownership requires the enclosed Certificate of Resolution,
signed by the same person it names; a list of corporate officers/partners/LLC members/HOA board is
filed for all facilities **every five years or on any change**. Workers' compensation and
disability proof must be one of the WCB forms — **ACORD is not accepted**: CE-200 (exemption),
C-105.2, U-26.3, SI-12 or GSI-105.2 for compensation; DB-120.1 or DB-155 for disability.
Non-refundable fees: **pool under 50 bathers $330, 50 or more $670, spa/whirlpool/wading
pool/spray ground $330, bathing beach $330**; cheque, money order or the enclosed credit-card form;
**cash not accepted**. A complete safety plan must accompany the application.

**Pages 3–4, the application.** Facility type ticks (indoor pool + capacity, outdoor pool +
capacity, indoor/outdoor wading pool, bathing beach, indoor/outdoor spa or whirlpool,
indoor/outdoor aquatic spray ground) · new establishment under construction · existing facility
change of ownership · previous facility name and permit number · name of establishment, phone,
address, municipality, state, zip · mailing name and address if different · owner's name, phone and
address · type of ownership (individual, partnership, corporation, unincorporated association,
municipality, LLC) · corporation officers, partners or LLC members with **home addresses** · proof
of workers' compensation and disability · **business hours, days closed, dates of operation if
seasonal** · source of water supply (public or central well) · sewage disposal (public sewer or
individual system) · garbage and refuse (public or private carter, name if private) · which safety
plans are included (swimming pool / bathing beach / recreational aquatic spray ground) · **required
e-mail address** and a second e-mail · agreement to comply with the county and state sanitary codes
· authorised signature, printed name, date, title · **FEI or SS #** (required by §5 of NY Tax Law).
Office-use block: date of inspection, date of approval, inspector's signature and employee number,
permit conditions, risk assessment, comments, BEQ permit information.

**Page 5, Certificate of Resolution for Authorization** (rev 3/2017) — corporation name, state of
organisation, date of the board meeting, the person authorised to execute and deliver the
application, officer's signature and title, corporate seal, and a **notary acknowledgement**
(state, county, date, notary public).

**Page 7, Credit Card Payment Authorization** — authorising a one-time charge: name, amount, a
four-row table of facility name / permit # or plan submittal / permit or plan fee, account type
(Visa, MasterCard, AMEX, Discover), cardholder name, **account number, security code, expiration
date, billing zip**, signature and date.

> **Never store or fill this page.** Card number, CVV and expiry are exactly the values the
> platform must not hold. It belongs in the packet as a blank the camp completes by hand.

## 12 · WCDOH — Children's Camp Original Permit Application Package
`westchester-camp-application.pdf` · 22 pages · rev 3/2025 · **no map** · holds six catalog
documents by page range

**Pages 1–3 are the county's own definition of a complete application** — the most useful two
pages in the whole set, because they say what a packet *is*:

> 1. Application for an Original Permit to Operate a Children's Camp · 2. Certificate of
> Resolution for Authorization (if a corporation; **must be notarized**) · 3. **Non-refundable
> application fee of $200.00**, if not fee exempt · 4. Workers' compensation and disability
> insurance (**ACORD not accepted**; CE-200, C-105.2, U-26.3, SI-12, GSI-105.2, DB-120.1,
> DB-155) · 5. **DOH-2271** director certified statement · 6. **LDSS-3370** SCR check ·
> 7. **DOH-367** · 8. **DOH-367a** · 9. **HD-91** amusement device survey · 10. **Safety plan
> attestations** · 11. **OEM camp contact form** · 12. **Self-inspection form** ·
> 13. **Complete safety plan and appropriate appendix**

Operating prior to inspection carries a fine and possible closure. Plans and specifications must
be approved **before construction or major renovation**.

### Pages 4–5 · Application for Original Children's Camp Permit
Permit type ticks: children's day camp · overnight camp · camp swimming pool · camp bathing beach
· **camp food service** · **camp frozen dessert** · camp aquatic spray ground. Water supply
public/private-onsite · sewage public/private-onsite · previously issued a permit (Yes/No) and the
prior name and permit ID · name of facility, phone, street address, municipality, state, zip ·
mailing name and address if different · owner's name (corporation name if a corporation), phone,
address · type of ownership (individual, partnership, corporation, unincorporated association,
municipality, LLC — corporation and unincorporated association must file the Certificate of
Resolution) · **corporation officers, partners, LLC members with home addresses**.

Then **operation information**: normal business hours · days of the week closed (seven ticks) ·
**opening and closing dates** · **number of campers** · **number of staff** · **total number of
staff including all volunteers** · water supply (public/central well) · sewage (public/individual)
· garbage and refuse (public, or private carter + name) · **name and address of pest control
operator**.

**Food manager's certification**: course taken Yes/No · name of the person who took it · course
certificate number · date of course. **Food allergen certification** (if permitted for food
service): number of certified staff · **names of certified staff**. **Required email address**.

Two agreements: to comply with the county and state sanitary codes, and to permit sampling of
ingredients, food, equipment, utensils and containers. Authorised signature · printed name · date
· title · **FEI # or SS #**, with ticks for "number applied for, but not yet received" and
"other". Office-use block: date of inspection, date of approval, inspector's signature and
employee number, permit conditions, risk assessment, comments.

### Page 6 · Certificate of Resolution for Authorization
Identical to the bathing-facility version: corporation name, state, board meeting date, offices,
the person authorised, what they are authorised to apply for, officer's signature and title,
corporate seal, and a notary acknowledgement.

### Page 7 · Credit Card Payment Authorization
The same card form as the bathing package — **card number, security code, expiry, billing zip**.
Never ours to store or fill.

### Pages 8–9 · DOH-367, pages 10–11 · DOH-367a
The state forms, bound into the county package. Identical to §1 and §2 above.

### Page 12 · HD-91 Children's Camp Amusement Device Survey
"Complete this survey for each amusement device." Devices are defined by **Part 45 of the
Department of Labor regulations**: carnival rides, go-carts, bumper boats, **water slides with a
vertical drop of 20 feet or more**, **climbing walls with mechanical belays**, **challenge
courses**, **zip lines**, **giant swings**.

Camp name · county (pre-printed Westchester) · a tick for **"No amusement devices available at the
camp."** · then a ten-row table: **amusement device type/name** (rope or challenge course elements
listed **separately**; for devices other than challenge course elements built on site, give the
**product manufacturer and serial number**) · **number available** · **amount of liability
insurance coverage** · **DOL permit (Yes/No)**. Person completing form · date · phone number.

### Pages 13–15 · Safety Plan Attestations — three separate signed forms
Each is print name · camp name · numbered acknowledgements · signature · date. **All trip leaders
must complete one**, so this is a per-person form, not a per-camp form.

- **Camp director** (5 points): has read and understands the safety plan including trip addendums ·
  every camper each season must be **swim assessed by a Progressive Swim Instructor** recognised
  by NYS DOH to be designated swimmer or non-swimmer · on a trip to a lifeguarded aquatics
  facility the camp must supply **one additional lifeguard per 25 campers/bathers**, in garments
  **identified with the camp's name** · the plan is kept onsite and available on request · **no
  change to the plan may be instituted until reviewed and approved by the county**.
- **Health director or on-site designee** (3 points): read and understood · kept onsite and
  available · no changes until county-approved.
- **Trip leader** (5 points): the same five as the camp director.

### Page 16 · DOH-2271
The state director certified statement, bound in. See §4.

### Pages 17–20 · LDSS-3370 — Statewide Central Register Database Check
The child-abuse register screening, **completed by the camp director for themselves, their spouse,
their children and any other person(s) in their home at the present time**.

Instructions (page 17): complete addresses for the **last 28 years**; name last-name-first; all
maiden, previous married, or alias names, circling which — **"NONE" if there are none**; a tick if
the applicant lives alone; every household member who lives with the applicant **whether related or
not, including adults, children and roommates**, with relationship, sex and date of birth;
**PO boxes are not acceptable**; countries and dates for time abroad, base names for military
service, **no unaccounted-for periods**; only the applicant signs; the signature must match the
name; and **the SCR will not accept a form signed more than 6 months ago**.

The form itself (page 18) collects: agency code, RID, CCFS number, category, phone, agency name,
agency liaison, agency street address, city, state, zip · a household table of relationship, last
name, first name, sex, date of birth · an address history table (current then previous, each with
street, apt, city, state, zip, from and to month/year) · the applicant's signature and date, plus
signatures for every household member **eighteen years of age or older**.

**Nothing in this document may ever be stored in the platform.** It is dates of birth and
28 years of address history for a director's spouse, children and roommates.

### Pages 21–22 · Department of Emergency Services O.E.M. Camp Contact Form
A separate form **per camp** if an organisation runs several. Camp organisation · street address ·
town or village · camp telephone · camp e-mail · **dates camp is in session** · actual location if
different from the mailing address.

**Pre-camp season contact**: contact, address, telephone, cell phone, e-mail.
**24-hour contact information (camp season)**, two people: contact, telephone, cell phone, pager,
e-mail.
**Camp statistics**: maximum number of children attending · number of staff or faculty ·
**handicapped or special needs children**.
**Transportation**: do you provide transportation · name of bus company · bus company contact ·
bus company phone · **are buses stored at the camp site during the day** · if not, **estimated
time to mobilise buses at camp** · **how long to return all campers home (early dismissal)** ·
number of private camp vehicles available · day trips planned.

> This is an emergency-management document, not a health document. Its questions are about how
> fast a camp can be evacuated — which is why the bus mobilisation and dismissal times are on it.

### Pages 19–20 · LDSS-3370 additional pages
Two continuation sheets, stapled to the form when its own space runs out: one for **previous
street addresses** (25 rows of address, city, state, zip, from and to month/year — "all dates must
be consecutive… be sure to associate address histories with particular individuals") and one for
**other household members**.

## 15 · What this means for where data should live

Read together, the 70 pages ask for far less *distinct* information than the cell counts suggest.
Almost everything is one of eight things, asked repeatedly:

1. **The camp as a legal entity** — facility name, facility code, permit number, street address,
   municipality, county, phone, e-mail (and a second e-mail), mailing address if different, owner
   name and address, ownership type, corporation officers/partners/LLC members **with home
   addresses**, FEI or SS number. Asked on both county applications, both bathing applications, the
   certificate of resolution and the OEM form. **One record, seven documents.**
2. **The season** — opening and closing dates, business hours, days closed, dates of operation,
   number of campers, number of staff, number of staff including volunteers, maximum children
   attending. Asked on DOH-367, DOH-367a, both county applications and the OEM form.
3. **People, with dates of birth** — camp director, health director (+ qualification and NYS
   licence), aquatics director, riflery instructor, food manager, allergen-certified staff, trip
   leaders, and every lifeguard, PSI and first-aid holder. The platform holds the roster; the forms
   want **name, title, date of birth, education, qualifying experience, licence number**.
4. **Certifications** — provider, course title, issue date, per person per certification type,
   with an expiry rule of its own for CPR ("may not exceed one year").
5. **Counts by category** — last season's attendance in 12 age/sex bands per session, counselors by
   age and sex, amusement devices, bathhouse fixtures.
6. **Physical plant** — water supply, sewage, garbage carrier, pest control operator, pool and beach
   dimensions and equipment, amusement devices with manufacturer, serial number, liability coverage
   and DOL permit.
7. **The written plan** — 76 camp components, 24 bathing components, indexed by page.
8. **Attestations and signatures** — six distinct signature blocks, of which three are per-person
   (director, health director, every trip leader) and one is notarised.

Two categories must **never** enter the platform: the LDSS-3370 household and 28-year address
history, and the DOH-2271 criminal-history statement — both are personal data about a director's
family, and both already fall under the module's `holds_personal_records` rule. The credit-card
authorization is a third.

And one structural fact should shape the whole design: **the county's renewal package says "change
any information that is incorrect."** The paperwork model that matches how camps actually work is a
standing record that gets corrected each season, not a form that gets filled each season.

# 16 · Is this comprehensive? No. Here is the gap register.

Checked 2026-08-31 against the county's live camp-operator page, the NYSDOH sex offender registry
fact sheet, and the module's own database. **The survey above is complete for the 22 documents the
catalog holds. It is not a complete account of what a Westchester camp must submit, show or
prove.** Each gap below is stated with how it was found, so it can be closed or dismissed on
evidence rather than re-argued.

## A · Documents the county requires that we do not hold — CONFIRMED

`https://health.westchestercountyny.gov/forms-and-permits/camp-operator` links thirteen documents
that are in neither the catalog nor this survey. A count of the 22 catalog documents matching any
of `injury / outbreak / abuse / rabies / epinephrine / incident / fire report / communicable / sex
offender / meningococcal` returns **zero**.

**Incident and outbreak reporting — an entire missing category.** These are not permit paperwork;
they are what a camp files *during* the season, on a clock (7-2.8(d) gives 24 hours for serious
injuries and abuse allegations, and the catalog has the rules — NY-0808, NY-2509, POOL-0701,
BEACH-0701 — but not the forms):

| Injury Report | DOH-61a |
| Illness Outbreak Report | DOH-61b |
| Allegation of Abuse Report | NYS-61 |
| Fire Report | NYS-61 Fire |
| Multiple Victim Injury Report | NYS-61h |
| Potential Rabies Exposure Report | NYS-61 Rabies |
| Epinephrine Administration | NYS-61e / DOH-61e |

**Also linked and not held:** the NYSDOH *Sex Offender Registry Search Procedures for Children's

the county's standalone credit-card payment form.

## B · The county asks for more than we record — CONFIRMED

All thirteen `NY-WESTCHESTER` requirements cite one source: "Children's Camp Application (2025)".
**The Westchester County Sanitary Code itself (Chapter 873, Article XVIII, Children's Summer Camps)
has never been read.** A county may impose duties beyond the state's, and at least one is already
visible on the county's page:

> "All camp staff, volunteers, **and persons that frequent the camp** must be run through the NYS
> Sex Offender Registry and evidence of such search must be maintained on-site" — and separately,
> through the **Dru Sjodin National Sex Offender Public Website advanced search**.

The state fact sheet (§7-2.5(l), PHL Article 13-B) requires the DCJS check for **employees and
volunteers**, before the first day and annually before arrival, and notes the **web-based DCJS
search does not satisfy the requirement** because it returns only Level 2 and 3 offenders.
Requirement NY-0504 encodes the state rule. It does **not** capture the county's two additions:
persons who merely frequent the camp, and the federal registry.

## C · Adjacent regimes the module deliberately does not track

`ScopeNote` already tells camps these are regulated separately and not tracked here, which is
honest — but "complete compliance home base" is a different promise from "camp permit home base",
and the difference is this list:

- **Food service** — 7-2.19 delegates entirely to **Part 14**, which has never been read; the
  county application has its own *Children's Camp Food Service* and *Frozen Dessert* permit ticks,
  a food manager's certification and a food allergen certification. Requirement NY-1901 is one row
  marked `needs_verification`.
- **Amusement devices** — HD-91 asks for a **DOL permit** per device (Part 45), a separate
  permitting track with its own inspections.
- **Building and fire code** — Uniform Code compliance, occupancy, local fire department inspection.
- **Drinking water** — a camp on its own well serving enough people is regulated as a public water
  system in its own right, beyond 7-2.6.
- **Employment** — child labour rules for staff under 18, annual sexual harassment prevention
  training, wage notices, paid sick leave.
- **Vehicles and drivers** — DOT inspections and Article 19-A qualification where they apply.

## D · Currency — partly verified

- **Verified good:** the county still publishes the **2025** original and renewal packets we hold,
  at the same URLs. Our copies are the current ones.
- **Not verified:** whether every bundled state form is the current revision. Markings range from
  DOH-1309 (1/93) to DOH-2436 (1/16); most are 2004–2012.
- **Not verified:** whether Subpart 7-2 has been amended since the text we encoded. The 78 state
  requirements were extracted from **Cornell LII** on 2026-08-29, every section stamped *NY State
  Register June 22, 2016, effective 6/22/2016* — not from the official NYSDOH publication.
  `regs.health.ny.gov` and `health.ny.gov` both refuse automated requests (403), so this needs a
  human with a browser, or a source that permits fetching.
- **No mechanism watches any of this.** Nothing in the product would notice a new packet, a revised
  form, or an amended section. For a product whose pitch is "shows you what changed this year",
  that mechanism is the feature, not an afterthought.

## E · Known encoding gaps, already documented

From `ny-subpart-7-2-extraction-notes.md`, carried here so they are not lost:

- Aquatics duties (NY-0510, NY-0511, NY-1101–NY-1104) are tagged `has_pool` only and **under-fire
  for a waterfront-only camp**; they should be `has_pool OR has_waterfront`.
- NY-2508/2509/2510 apply only to camps *for children with developmental disabilities*, a narrower
  class than the `enrolls_campers_with_disabilities` flag they use, so they **over-fire**.
- `water_source` has two values but 7-2.6 recognises three cases (groundwater, surface water or
  GWUDI, off-site public supply).
- A strict reading of Subpart 7-2 yields roughly **110 distinct duties**; the catalog deliberately
  consolidates to 78.
- Sections excluded by choice: 7-2.1 enforcement, 7-2.2 definitions, 7-2.3 applicability, 7-2.12
  construction, 7-2.13 location, 7-2.14 housing maintenance, 7-2.15 heat/light/ventilation, 7-2.17
  electrical, 7-2.24 variance. Several are one-time or construction duties, but 7-2.12's **60-day
  pre-construction notice** is a real filing that a camp doing work over the winter owes.

## F · What comprehensive would take

1. Read **Westchester Sanitary Code Article XVIII** end to end, the way Subpart 7-2 was read.
2. Add the incident-reporting documents as a **document class of their own** — they are filed on a
   clock during the season, not with the permit, and the module has no concept for that yet.
3. Read **Part 14** and decide whether food service is in scope or explicitly out.
4. Re-verify all 25 sections of Subpart 7-2 against the official NYSDOH text and record the
   amendment date each was read at.
5. Give every document and requirement a **`source_checked_on` date and a content hash**, so
   "what changed this year" is computed rather than remembered.
6. Close the five encoding gaps in §E.
