# CampCommand Privacy Policy


**Effective date:** July 24, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction and Scope

CampCommand is a camp-management platform used by summer camps, retreat centers, and similar organizations ("Camps") to run their day-to-day operations. This Privacy Policy explains what information CampCommand handles, why, how it is protected, and the choices and rights that apply to it.

This policy covers:

- The **CampCommand web application** and **iOS application** used by Camp staff and administrators.
- The **public guest portal** used by retreat guest-group coordinators.
- The **public facility-issue reporting** feature that lets anyone report a maintenance or safety issue at a Camp.

A key thing to understand up front: **CampCommand is a tool that Camps use to manage their own records.** Camp staff enter the information in the system. CampCommand does **not** collect information directly from campers or their families over the internet, and campers do not have accounts or log in.

Because of this structure, each Camp and CampCommand play different legal roles, which we explain next.

---

## 2. Definitions: Who Is Responsible for the Data

- **"Controller"** means the party that decides what personal information is collected and why. **Each Camp is the Controller** of the information about its staff, campers, guests, and operations. The Camp decides what to enter, whose data to hold, and how it is used within its organization.

- **"Processor"** means the party that stores and processes personal information on the Controller's behalf and under its instructions. **CampCommand is the Processor.** We host the data, keep it secure, and make it available to the Camp's authorized staff. We do not use Camp data for our own independent purposes.

- **"You" / "Customer"** in this policy generally refers to the Camp organization that licenses CampCommand.

- **"Personal information"** (or "personal data") means information that identifies or relates to an identifiable individual.

- **"Subprocessor"** means a third party we engage to help provide the service (for example, our hosting provider).

If you are a camper, parent or guardian, staff member, or guest and you have questions about how a particular Camp uses your information, the Camp is the Controller and is the right first point of contact. We will support any Camp in responding to such requests.

## 3. Information We Handle and Why

Camp staff enter and manage the following categories of information in CampCommand. We handle each category solely to provide the platform's operational features to the Camp.

### 3.1 Camp staff accounts
- **What:** Name and email address of staff and administrators who have logins; role and permission assignments; group memberships that control what each staff member can see.
- **Why:** To authenticate users, control access, and attribute actions within the platform.

### 3.2 Camper records (largely minors)
- **What:** Camper name, cabin/group assignment, and free-text notes entered by staff.
- **Why:** To help Camps organize campers, assignments, and day-to-day operations. See **Section 5** for how we treat minors' data.

### 3.3 Camper health and dietary information (sensitive)
- **What:** Allergies and dietary restrictions, restriction severity (up to and including anaphylactic), related notes, and uploaded nurse/allergy documents (PDFs). These documents may contain medications, diagnoses, and emergency-contact details.
- **Why:** To help Camps keep campers safe, for example, so kitchen and nursing staff can respect allergies and dietary needs. This is treated as sensitive information with heightened protections. See **Section 5**.

### 3.4 Retreat and guest-group information
- **What:** Contact details for guest-group coordinators (for example, the organizer of a visiting retreat), and information those coordinators submit through the public guest portal about their group's stay.
- **Why:** To let Camps manage external group rentals and to let coordinators complete tasks (such as providing rooming or logistics details) through a limited, token-scoped portal.

### 3.5 Public facility-issue reports
- **What:** A description of a maintenance or safety issue, an optional photo, and (at the reporter's choice) an optional name and contact detail.
- **Why:** To let anyone flag a problem at a facility so Camp staff can address it. Reporter identity is optional; a report can be submitted anonymously.

### 3.6 Operational and financial records
- **What:** Records generated by running the Camp, including pool and water-safety logs, safety records, assets, building systems, commissary/food and inventory, and retreat records including financial/payment records.
- **Why:** To provide the platform's operational modules. **Note:** CampCommand records that payments occurred and related amounts, but **does not store credit-card or bank-account numbers.** Card processing, where it occurs, happens outside CampCommand.

### 3.7 AI-assisted features
- **What:** Certain images and documents are sent to our AI subprocessor for automated reading, specifically, **pool test-strip photos** (to read chemical readings) and **allergy/health documents** (to help extract structured information).
- **Why:** To save staff time and reduce transcription errors. See **Section 6** for details on this sharing.

### 3.8 Technical and log information
- **What:** Standard operational data such as authentication events, audit logs of sensitive actions, and system logs needed to operate and secure the service.
- **Why:** Security, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and accountability.

We do **not** sell personal information, and we do **not** use camper, health, guest, or Camp operational data for advertising.

## 4. Legal Bases (Brief)

Where data-protection laws such as the EU/UK GDPR apply, the **Camp (as Controller)** is responsible for establishing the legal basis for its collection and use of personal information. Typical bases a Camp may rely on include:

- **Contract / legitimate operations**, running the camp program the individual or their family enrolled in.
- **Legitimate interests**, operating, securing, and improving camp operations in ways individuals would reasonably expect.
- **Consent**, for example, a facility-issue reporter voluntarily providing their name and contact.
- **Vital interests / health and safety**, handling allergy and medical information to protect a camper's health.
- **Legal obligation**, where the Camp must retain certain records.

**CampCommand, as Processor,** processes this information under its contract with the Camp and according to the Camp's instructions.

## 5. Children's Data and Health Data (Special Care)

This section deserves special attention because CampCommand holds information about **minors**, including sensitive **health information**.

### 5.1 How children's data enters the system
CampCommand does **not** collect personal information directly from children online. Children do not have accounts, do not log in, and are not asked to submit information through the product. Instead, **Camp staff enter camper information** as part of managing the Camp.

### 5.2 COPPA context (honest framing)
The U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA") applies to operators that collect personal information **online from children under 13**. Because CampCommand does not collect information online from children (staff enter it) the classic COPPA "operator collecting from a child" obligations may not attach to CampCommand in the usual way. **This is a legal question that must be confirmed by counsel** and may depend on jurisdiction and how a given Camp uses the product. Regardless of the precise legal characterization, we treat minors' data with heightened care.

### 5.3 Heightened care for camper and health data
Because we hold minors' records and sensitive health information, we apply extra protections:

- **Fail-closed health access:** Camper health and dietary information is hidden by default. It is visible **only** to Camp administrators or to staff who have been explicitly cleared to view it. If permissions are ambiguous, the system denies access rather than granting it.
- **Role-based access:** Staff see only what their role and group permissions allow.
- **Tenant isolation:** One Camp's data is technically isolated from every other Camp's data (see **Section 8**).
- **Audit logging:** Access to and changes involving sensitive records are logged.
- **Minimization:** We ask Camps to upload only what they need, and health documents are stored and access-controlled like the sensitive data they are.

Parents and guardians with questions about a specific child's information should contact the **Camp** (the Controller). We will assist the Camp in fulfilling access, correction, and deletion requests.

## 6. How Data Is Shared, and Our Subprocessors

We do not sell personal information and do not share it except as needed to run the service or as required by law. Specifically, data may be shared:

- **With the Camp's own authorized users**, according to their roles and permissions.
- **With our subprocessors**, listed below, strictly to provide the service.
- **When required by law**, such as a valid legal request, or to protect safety and rights.
- **In a business transfer** (e.g., merger or acquisition), subject to this policy and applicable law.

### 6.1 Subprocessors

| Subprocessor | Role | What it handles |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Database, authentication, and file storage hosting (runs on Amazon Web Services / AWS) | Stores CampCommand's data, manages logins, and stores uploaded files such as health documents and photos. |
| **Anthropic** | AI features (Claude) | Receives specific images/documents (pool test-strip photos and allergy/health documents) to automatically read them and return structured results. |

We may update this list as the service evolves; material changes will be reflected here (see **Section 10**).

### 6.2 Note on AI processing
Only the specific content needed for a feature is sent to our AI subprocessor (Anthropic) (for example, a test-strip photo or an allergy document) and only to produce a result for the Camp (such as reading chemical levels or extracting allergy details). The AI is used as a tool to assist Camp staff; the Camp remains the Controller of the underlying records.

## 7. Data Retention

- We retain a Camp's data **for the life of the customer relationship**, so the Camp can continue to operate and access its historical records.
- If a Camp requests **deletion** of specific records or of its account, we delete the corresponding data (subject to any limited retention required by law or for legitimate security and backup cycles).
- Uploaded health documents and photos follow the same retention approach as the records they belong to.
- Certain minimal logs may be retained for security and accountability for a limited period.

A Camp can request export or deletion at any time (see **Section 9**).

---

## 8. Security

We take the sensitivity of camper, health, and operational data seriously and apply the following controls:

- **Database-level tenant isolation:** Each Camp is a separate tenant, enforced at the database layer using PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS), so one Camp cannot access another Camp's data.
- **Role-based access control:** Staff access is limited by role and by group-based module permissions.
- **Fail-closed sensitive data:** Camper health data is hidden unless a user is an administrator or explicitly cleared; ambiguous cases default to "no access."
- **Encryption in transit and at rest:** Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest.
- **Least-privilege guest portal:** The public guest portal is scoped by a limited access token and has no direct database access; it exposes only what a given guest coordinator needs.
- **Audit logging:** Sensitive actions are logged for accountability.
- **Secure infrastructure:** Data is hosted on **Supabase**, which maintains **SOC 2 Type II** compliance and runs on AWS infrastructure.

**Important honesty note:** These certifications describe our **infrastructure provider**. CampCommand itself does not claim to be independently SOC 2, HIPAA, or GDPR "certified." We build on SOC 2-compliant infrastructure and follow equivalent security practices, but no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your Rights: Access, Export, Correction, and Deletion

The rights available depend on applicable law and on your relationship to the data. Because each Camp is the Controller, most individual requests (from campers, parents, guests, or staff) should go to the relevant **Camp**, which can act using the tools below. CampCommand supports these capabilities:

- **Access and export:** CampCommand provides a **per-Camp data export**, so a Camp can obtain a copy of its data.
- **Correction:** Information entered into CampCommand is **editable** by authorized staff, so records can be corrected or updated.
- **Deletion:** Data can be **deleted** on request, subject to limited legal or security-related retention.

If you are an individual (for example, a parent asking about your child's record or a staff member asking about your account) and you are not sure who to contact, reach out to your Camp first. You may also contact us at eric@campcommand.app and we will route your request appropriately and assist the Camp as its Processor.

Where required by law, you may also have rights to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority.

## 10. International Considerations (Brief)

CampCommand's data is hosted and processed in the United States (Supabase on AWS, US region; Anthropic, United States), which may be located in, or accessed from, countries other than where a Camp or an individual is located. Where data is transferred across borders, such transfers are handled in accordance with applicable law, which may include appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (where applicable). Camps that operate in regions with specific data-residency or transfer requirements should confirm suitability for their needs; specifics should be confirmed with counsel for the State of Delaware, United States.
