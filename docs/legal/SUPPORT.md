# CampCommand support

Help for camp staff using the CampCommand iPhone app and web app.

## Get in touch

Email **[eric@campcommand.app](mailto:eric@campcommand.app)**. We answer within one business
day, and faster during the summer season when camps are running.

When you write, it helps to include your camp's name, the screen you were on, and what you
expected to happen.

## I downloaded the app but I can't sign in

This is the most common question, and it usually isn't a fault.

CampCommand accounts are created by your camp, not by signing up in the app. Your camp
administrator sends an invitation link, or gives you a join code to enter on the sign-in
screen. Until your camp has invited you, there is no account to sign in to.

If your camp already uses CampCommand, ask whoever runs it, normally the camp director or
facilities manager, to send you an invitation. If your camp doesn't use it yet and you'd like
to, email us and we'll set up a walkthrough.

## I have an account but the code never arrives

By default we email you a one-time sign-in code rather than asking for a password. If it hasn't
appeared within a minute or two:

- check the junk or spam folder
- confirm the address you entered is the one your camp invited
- choose **Use a password instead** on the sign-in screen if you've set a password

Still stuck? Email us and we'll sort it out directly.

## Something in the app looks wrong

Tell us what you saw and roughly when. Screenshots help a great deal. If it relates to a
specific issue, pool reading or checklist item, mentioning its title lets us find the exact
record.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: open **Profile**, then **Delete my account**.
This removes your sign-in, your profile and your camp memberships.

The work you logged stays with the camp, because it's the camp's operating record rather than
your personal data, a health inspector asking for a season of pool readings shouldn't find a
gap where someone's summer was. Those records are detached from your account and keep only the
name they were already stamped with.

If you're the only administrator of a camp, the app will ask you to make someone else an
administrator first, so the camp isn't left without anyone who can run it.
