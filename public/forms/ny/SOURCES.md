## Pool and bathing-beach coverage gap (10 NYCRR Subparts 6-1 / 6-2)

A camp with a pool or waterfront is a regulated bathing facility as well as a camp. On the
Westchester application (p. 4) the camp checks "Children's Camp Swimming Pool" / "Children's
Camp Bathing Beach" / "Children's Camp Aquatic Spray Ground", so the pool permit rides along
with the camp permit. Beyond DOH-2286 (bundled) the following are not bundled — they are not
part of the camp permit packet, but a camp with water will encounter them:

- DOH-1323, Report on Operation of Swimming Pool — https://www.health.ny.gov/forms/doh-1323.pdf (daily operations log, Subpart 6-1)
- DOH-2287, Daily Report on Beach Operations — https://www.health.ny.gov/forms/doh-2287.pdf (Subpart 6-2)
- DOH-1309, Engineering Report for Swimming Pool Plans — https://www.health.ny.gov/forms/doh-1309.pdf (new construction / major alteration)
- DOH-2436, Engineering Report for Bathing Beaches — https://www.health.ny.gov/forms/doh-2436.pdf
- WCDOH Bathing Facility Original Application (2025) — https://health.westchestercountyny.gov/images/stories/Environmental%20Forms/Pool%20Beach/WCDOH%20BATHING%20FACILITY%20ORIGINAL%20APPLICATION%202025.pdf (used when the facility is permitted separately from the camp)
- WCDOH Certification that Bathing Facility Safety Plans Are Up To Date (2025) — https://health.westchestercountyny.gov/images/stories/Environmental%20Forms/Pool%20Beach/WCDOH%20CERTIFICATION%20THAT%20BATHING%20FACILITY%20SAFETY%20PLANS%20ARE%20UP%20TO%20DATE%202025.pdf

Also on the county camp-operator page, incident reporting forms (DOH-61a injury, DOH-61b
illness outbreak, NYS-61 allegation of abuse, NYS-61 fire, NYS-61h multiple-victim injury,
NYS-61 rabies, DOH-61e epinephrine) are linked from nyc.gov and other county mirrors rather
than health.ny.gov; not bundled.

### When a camp actually uses these

A camp pool or waterfront is normally permitted **with the camp**, on the children's camp
application, because 6-1.3(b) and 6-2.3(b) exempt a camp bathing facility from the separate
permit sections 6-1.5 and 6-2.5. So the bathing facility application above is for a
facility permitted on its own, not the usual camp path. The safety plan certification is
the substitute for resubmitting a plan that has not changed since last season. Both
distinctions are recorded in `obtain_note` on the matching `compliance_authority_forms`
rows, and the reasoning is in `docs/compliance/ny-bathing-extraction-notes.md`.

The incident reporting forms listed in the gap section above (DOH-61a, DOH-61b, NYS-61,
NYS-61h, DOH-61e) are still not bundled; nothing in this pass changed that.
