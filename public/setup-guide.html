Locations

  • Every building and what it isCabin 1–12 · Dining Hall · Bathhouse
  • Rooms inside each buildingCabin 4 → bunkroom, counselor room, bathroom
  • Bed count for rooms people sleep in, and whether each is step-free

Team

  • One camp administrator — name and email. They invite everyone else and set who sees what.

Season

  • Opening and closing dates plus your ACA or health inspection date if booked
  • Session dates with camper and staff countsSession 1 · Jun 14–27 · 180 campers, 55 staff

By module

Only the ones you are turning on

Issues & Repairs

Nothing to send. Runs on your locations and team.

Pre/Post Camp

  • Opening checklistturn on water · open pool · test alarms · mow fields
  • Closing checklistblow out lines · pull docks · winterize kitchen

Safety & Compliance

  • Fire extinguishers, smoke and CO alarms — location, last inspected, how often, vendor
  • Kitchen hood — last cleaned, how often, vendor
  • Drills and trainings you run and how oftenfire · lost camper · lockdown · severe weather
  • Staff certifications with expiry datesLifeguard · CPR/AED · First Aid · ServSafe
  • Licenses and permits with expiry datescamp operating permit · food service · ACA · boiler

Pool & Waterfront

  • Each body of water and whether it is a pool or a natural waterfront
  • Inspection schedule — your health department sheet works
  • Trackable equipment — pumps, filters, heaters, chlorinators, last serviced
  • Pool opening and closing steps

Assets & Vehicles

  • Vehicles — make, model, year, plate, mileage, registration expiry
  • Golf carts, utility vehicles, mowers — make, model, engine hours
  • Watercraft — registration and expiry, capacity, lifejacket count
  • Technology — item, model, serial, who holds itlaptops · tablets · radios · cameras

Building Systems

  • Main shutoffs per building — water, electrical panel, gasDining Hall: water under 3-bay sink, panel in back hall
  • Panel schedules — a photo of the panel door is enough
  • Winterization steps per building

Room-by-room outlets and fixtures come later. Your crew adds them from their phones.

Kitchen Manager

  • Last year's opening order — the invoice or vendor order guide. Does more than anything else on this list.
  • Vendors — account number, rep, order cutoff, delivery day, minimum
  • Recipes or menu rotation — ingredients, quantities, how many it serves
  • Ordering rhythm — count day, order day, delivery day, days each order covers
  • Minimum quantities for items you never want to run out of
  • Camper and staff allergies — name, session, need, severity

Retreat Manager

  • Which rooms and spaces you rent out with bed counts
  • Your rate card — per person per night, per cabin, or flat fee — plus your deposit rule
  • Your retreat agreement as a PDF or Word file

Sending it to us

Sign in→Camp Info→Setup Files→Drop files in

Any format. Spreadsheets, PDFs, Word, photos of a binder page or panel door, or a ZIP of all of it. There is no template to fill in. Send in as many batches as you like. Please send anything with camper information through this upload rather than by email.
